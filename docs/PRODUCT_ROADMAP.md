# Product roadmap

This repository is the interactive mock. The phases below are the product sequence. “In this build” means a person can click it today. It does not mean the service behind it exists.

## In this build

- Signed-in shell for one member, with an editable profile and photo
- Community feed with compose, likes, and threat-tagged posts
- Five groups, join and leave, trust floors on the room page
- Four academy paths and on-device progress
- CAFP certificate art, preview checkout, download, and local verify
- Trust dashboard (standing is a stored number, not a live score)
- Notifications, scripted Copilot, events list, explore, about
- Live tech-news wire with a seeded fallback
- Job search that links out and lists sample openings
- Desktop nav and the mobile five-tab bar

## Still ahead

**Phase 1 leftovers.** Accounts, a server for posts and progress, profiles for members other than the signed-in founder, and a trust score that moves when reviews and incidents do. Likes, joins, and the feed currently die with the tab.

**Phase 2 — Events and mentorship.** The calendar is three static cards. Mentorship is not in the UI.

**Phase 3 — Labs and marketplace.** Not started. Explore’s “673 projects” figure is copy on the home stats, not a project index.

**Phase 4 — Guardian integration.** The trust page and the footer name Guardian. There is no separate Guardian product wired in. Weaponizing a score is described as an offense; there is no report flow.

**Payments.** CAFP prices are the intended public prices. The card form does not call a processor.

**Copilot.** Replies are pattern matches in `replyTo`. A model-backed copilot would still be expected to stay on this network: trust, groups, courses, and the feed.
